1. What we store

  • A device-generated account identifier and session keys.

2. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no third-party analytics, no tracking SDKs.

3. Storage & security

Data is stored on this server only. As a community service, no guarantee of security or availability is provided.

4. Deletion

You may request deletion of your account data from the server operators at any time.
